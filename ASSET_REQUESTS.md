# Moubon V13 — Remaining Asset Requests

The hero painterly images, boat cut-out, bank panels, water texture and foreground reeds are included and optimized. The six five-second River Story MP4s have now been integrated; their original painting sources remain archived under `docs/source-art/`. The items below remain owner-supplied; they are intentionally not fabricated.

## 1. Real product photography — required before publishing products

For each actual product:
- 3–5 photos per product, preferably 1200×1200 px or larger.
- WebP or AVIF, sRGB, clean background and one lifestyle image if available.
- Include a front-facing label photo where the actual label is legible.
- Supply the exact approved product name, net weight/volume, package options, price and variant prices.
- Save photos under `public/assets/` and set `published: true` only after content, price and photo are approved.

## 2. Business and order details — required

- Official WhatsApp Business number with country code, digits only.
- Delivery coverage, delivery time and delivery charge rules.
- Actual payment instructions for bKash/Nagad, if offered; confirm account type and number.
- Confirm whether Cash on Delivery is actually offered.
- Official business name, contact email/phone and physical/business address if they should be published.
- Official Facebook/Instagram pages and Messenger page URL, if available.
- Real HTTPS domain for `SITE_URL` in `config.js`.
- Approved return and delivery policy wording.

## 3. Verifiable trust information — optional

Only provide facts that can be substantiated, such as an actual licence, lab report, traceable source or certification. Supply exact wording, issuer, document/photo evidence, date/validity and permission to publish. The trust strip remains hidden until real items are configured.

## 4. Audio — owner verification required

- `public/assets/tiger-growl-demo.wav` is a sample/demo sound. Replace it with an owned/licensed tiger audio file or confirm its commercial-use licence in writing before commercial use.
- No ambient audio toggle is shown in V13. If ambient audio is wanted later, supply an owned/licensed seamless local recording (15–45 seconds) and request that the feature be re-enabled.

## 5. Story video rights and optional higher-resolution masters

- The six supplied five-second, 1280×720 MP4 clips are integrated in the current website.
- Confirm that the generated clips may be used on the public website and in commercial promotion under the applicable Google Flow/service terms.
- If higher-resolution masters become available later, provide the same six clips at 1920×1080 or 4K, 24–30 fps, with the same framing and character continuity. The current website copies are optimized H.264 files and do not require replacements to function.

## 6. Hero depth layers — optional

If a true multi-plane parallax hero is desired, supply the approved hero scene split into transparent layers: background sky/distant forest, mid-ground mangroves/water, foreground leaves/honeycomb, and boat/collectors. Recommended 1920×1080 minimum, 16:9, PNG or WebP with alpha. No layer separation should be guessed from a flattened image if it damages the original scene.

## 7. Font preference — optional

The current body font is self-hosted Noto Sans Bengali. If the exact Hind Siliguri family is a brand requirement, supply official regular and bold Bengali/Latin WOFF2 subsets plus its SIL Open Font License notice.

## 8. Packaging and brand assets — optional

- Actual product jar/bottle and label artwork (SVG/PDF/AI or high-resolution PNG).
- Brand logo source, colour codes and packaging photos.
- Confirmed Bengali and English brand spelling.
